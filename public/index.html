Who we are

Orient Palace Contracting & Building Maintenance LLC was established in Dubai in 1997 and provides construction, civil maintenance and MEP services under one roof.

Our engineers, supervisors and technicians have delivered projects in commercial and residential buildings, apartments, hospitals, hotels, shopping malls and warehouses: building maintenance, electrical, plumbing and sanitary, ceilings and partitions, painting, flooring and tiling, air-conditioning, carpentry and MEP works.
