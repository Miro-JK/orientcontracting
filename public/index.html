Introduction

Orient Palace Contracting & Building Maintenance LLC, established in 1997, is a leading company in Dubai, UAE, providing all types of construction, civil maintenance and MEP services under one roof.

We are a professional team of engineers, technicians and well-experienced labourers delivering quality service in everything we build. Over the years we have completed numerous projects in commercial and residential buildings, apartments, hospitals, hotels, shopping malls and warehouses.

We specialise in building maintenance, electrical services, plumbing and sanitary, ceilings and partitions, plaster and painting, flooring and tiling, air-conditioning and ventilation, carpentry and MEP works. All of it is carried out by highly qualified and technically competent engineers, supervisors and technicians, on projects of every size for commercial, residential and industrial use.

We hold ourselves to the highest standards of work and safety, adopting best practices to protect our employees. We believe in using the best technology and skilled manpower for your needs, backed by continuous research and development.

Our team's decades of experience are the inner strength of our company. We are passionate about our work, committed to excellence, and continue to build lasting relationships with our customers and the community we serve.

Our commitment reflects our loyalty to every client. On completed projects we have a reputation for optimum performance and for meeting tight deadlines with an exceptional work attitude.
