About us

Built on decades of hands-on work.

Serving Dubai and the UAE since 1997.

Introduction

A Dubai contractor you can rely on.

Established in 1997, Orient Palace Contracting & Building Maintenance LLC provides all types of construction, civil maintenance and MEP services under one roof. Over the years we have delivered projects across commercial and residential buildings, apartments, hospitals, hotels, shopping malls and warehouses.

Every job is handled by qualified engineers, supervisors and technicians who hold to high standards of workmanship and safety. Our reputation across the UAE comes from quality work, competitive pricing, dependable support, and meeting tight deadlines.
